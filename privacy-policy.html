Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 14, 2026.

TrashIt collects a one-way hashed version of your communication data solely to enforce the 24-hour posting limit and prevent abuse. The hashed info is deleted every 3 days. This hash cannot be used to identify you and is never shared with third parties.

What We Collect

  • Only the text of your rant and hashed communication info.
  • Fully private usage analytics - no tracking cookies or advertising IDs.

How We Use It

To display rants in the app and calculate the weekly “most trashed” word/phrase.

Security

All data is encrypted on Supabase using industry-standard practices.
