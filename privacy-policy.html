Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2025

TrashIt collects a one-way hashed version of your IP address solely to enforce the 24-hour voting limit and prevent abuse. This hash cannot be used to identify you and is never shared with third parties.

What We Collect

  • Only the text of your rant and a hashed IP.
  • Fully private usage analytics - no tracking cookies or advertising IDs.

How We Use It

To display rants in the app and calculate the weekly “most trashed” word/phrase.

Security

All data is encrypted on Supabase using industry-standard practices.
